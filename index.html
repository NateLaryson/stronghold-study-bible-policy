Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Stronghold Study Bible ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Stronghold Study Bible mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account or log in, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • User account credentials

Location Information

With your permission, the app may collect your location information to provide location-based features and improve user experience.

Photos and Media

The app may access photos and media files that you choose to upload or share within the application.

Files and Documents

The app may access files and documents selected by you for upload, sharing, or storage within the app.

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect certain information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • IP address

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users through Firebase Authentication.
  • Provide Bible study tools and app features.
  • Store and manage user-generated content.
  • Improve app functionality and performance.
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests.
  • Maintain app security and prevent abuse.

Firebase Services

Stronghold Study Bible uses Google Firebase services, which may collect information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Firebase may process data such as device identifiers, analytics data, authentication information, and diagnostic information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers required to operate the application.
  • When required by law or legal process.
  • To protect our rights, users, or the public.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Update or correct your information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw permissions granted to the application.

Children's Privacy

Stronghold Study Bible is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
